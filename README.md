# Vijaykanth Bonkuri — Research Portfolio

M.S. student in Biotechnology and Graduate Research Assistant at West Virginia State University, working under Dr. David Huber.

## Research focus

- **River sediment & AMD microbiomes:** Temperature-driven shifts in methanogenic and methanotrophic communities in Kanawha River sediments, alongside collaborative acid mine drainage remediation research.
- **Plant-associated microbiomes:** Bacterial and fungal community composition and predicted functional potential, including cotton rhizosphere reanalysis using K-means clustering.
- **AI/ML in microbial ecology:** Unsupervised pattern discovery and supervised ecological prediction when suitable labeled data are available.

## Future direction

Agricultural microbiology, plant–microbe interactions, plant pathology, microbiome bioinformatics, and machine learning.
